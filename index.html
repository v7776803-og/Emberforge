Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. Emberforge runs in your browser and your projects are stored locally.

Where do my API keys go?

Keys stay in your browser and are only sent to the provider you configured. They are never exported or logged.

How do I install it?

Download Emberforge, then open it in a desktop browser. There is nothing to install, and you can try the demo without configuring anything.

Can it work completely offline?

Yes. Connect a local model through Ollama or LM Studio and the editor, preview and agents all run without internet.

Can the AI change files without asking?

Only if you turn on Auto-apply. Otherwise every change arrives as a diff you accept or reject.

  • Seven AI agents that plan, build, test and review.

Requirements

  • A screen at least 1100px wide.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. You only pay your own model provider if you choose a cloud model.

Does it send my code anywhere?

Only to the model provider you configure, and only the context you choose. Local models keep everything on your machine.

Why do I confirm the terms first?

So you know how Emberforge handles your data before you start.
