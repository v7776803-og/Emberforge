Get Emberforge

AI that shows its work.

Emberforge is a coding workstation built on one idea: you should always be able to see, check and undo what an AI does to your code.

Our story

AI can write a lot of code quickly. The hard part is trusting it. We built Emberforge so you can use that speed without giving up control: agents plan in the open, changes arrive as diffs, and nothing is written until you approve it.

Transparent

Every action, file read and change is visible, with the model and provider that produced it.

In your control

You choose the model, the context and whether any change is applied.

Local first

Your projects and chats live in your browser. Local models keep everything on your machine.

Feedback, bug reports and ideas are welcome. We read every message.
